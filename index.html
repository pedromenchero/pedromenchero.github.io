About

I design and integrate ServiceNow platforms for multinational and local enterprises, and lead the teams that build them. My work sits between architecture and delivery: setting technical governance and platform standards, designing integrations, and working with C-level executives to shape solutions around how their business actually runs.

I’ve directed large, multicultural teams in four countries, and I mentor developers and consultants in ServiceNow best practice.
